# Waldo Design System

## Overview

Waldo is a personal finance tracker: wallets, transactions, categories, a
dashboard. Its visual identity is deliberately quiet - a warm cream canvas
and near-black ink, almost no color, and generous pill-shaped rounding. The
app wants to feel calm and trustworthy rather than chart-y or alarmist, which
is why the single accent color (a burnt orange) is reserved strictly for
errors and negative amounts: when orange appears, it always means "pay
attention," never "this is a nice highlight."

The departures from the flat ink/cream system are physical materials. The
dashboard's balance pouch and the undo bar are dark leather with cream text
in both themes, the way a real leather wallet doesn't change color because
the room got darker. Each wallet card renders on a metal finish
(platinum/gold/bronze): a photo in light mode, a true dark-metal gradient in
dark mode rather than the same photo dimmed down. Because the metal itself
changes, its ink follows it - `leather` ink on the light photo, `on-leather`
ink on the dark metal.

## Colors

- **Surface (`#FCFBFA` light / `#1F1D1B` dark):** The page canvas color -
  "Lifted Cream" in light mode, a warm near-black in dark mode. Cards,
  dialogs, sheets, and list items all sit on this.
- **On Surface (`#141413` light / `#F3F0EE` dark):** Primary text and icon
  color on top of `surface`.
- **Surface Container (`#F3F0EE` light / `#141413` dark):** One step away
  from `surface` - used as the `Scaffold` background behind cards, so cards
  visibly lift off the page.
- **On Surface Variant (`#696969` light / `#A39E97` dark):** Muted secondary
  text - dates, captions, placeholders, dividers at low alpha. As text it is
  used at full strength (at least 4.8:1 on any surface); a faded copy would
  drop placeholders and captions under WCAG's 4.5:1.
- **Primary (`#141413` light / `#F3F0EE` dark):** The CTA color - ink black
  in light mode, inverts to cream in dark mode. Used for filled buttons, the
  FAB, and the active nav indicator.
- **On Primary (`#F3F0EE` light / `#141413` dark):** Text/icon color on top
  of a `primary`-filled surface.
- **Primary Strong (`#000000` light / `#FFFFFF` dark):** A punchier tonal
  step beyond `primary` for the few places that need to read as the single
  most emphasized thing on a plain surface - income amounts, the chart's
  income series, and the date picker's "today" marker. It's the same ink/cream hue family as `primary`, just
  pushed to the far end (pure black / pure white) rather than a new color,
  which keeps Waldo's "almost no color" rule intact while still giving these
  specific moments real visual weight. It's a token for *emphasis on a plain
  surface*, not a substitute for `primary` on filled buttons or chips.
- **Primary Container (`#F4F4F4` light / `#2A2724` dark):** A soft tonal fill
  for selected/active chips and segmented controls.
- **Error (`#C44100` light / `#F37338` dark):** The single accent hue in the
  entire system. Used only for destructive actions and negative/expense
  amounts - never decoratively. The light value is dark enough for both of
  its jobs to reach 4.5:1: as text on the page (4.51:1) and under
  `on-primary` text on a filled button (4.51:1).
- **Field Fill / Field Border (`#F5F5F5`/`#EEEEEE` light,
  `#242422`/`#353330` dark):** A neutral gray, deliberately *not* tinted
  toward the warm cream palette - text inputs need to read as "a field,"
  visually distinct from the cream cards and page background around them.
- **Leather / On Leather (`#141413` / `#F3F0EE`, same in both themes):** The
  leather material and its cream text: the balance pouch and the undo bar.
  Fixed across light and dark because they model a physical object, not a
  themed surface. Wallet cards borrow the same two colors as ink
  (`walletCardInkColor`), picking whichever reads on the current finish.

## Typography

Everything is set in the **platform's system font** - SF Pro on iOS, Roboto
on Android - so nothing is bundled or downloaded. The base system has two weights - `500` for
anything that reads as a heading/label (with tight, slightly negative
tracking), and `400` for body copy (with a touch of negative tracking to
compensate for the lack of a true 450 weight) - plus four heavier "strong"
steps (`600`/`700`/`800`/`900`) reserved for the handful of places that need
to visually dominate the screen: transaction amounts, card titles, and the
balance figure on the dashboard.

- **Headline Display (28px / 500):** Reserved as the base for the dashboard
  balance figure's weight ladder (see `headline-display-black` below).
- **Headline Large / Medium (22px/16px / 500):** Card and section titles.
- **Body Large / Medium / Small (16px/14px/12px / 400):** Paragraph and
  list-item copy.
- **Label Large / Medium / Small (14px/12px/11px / 500):** Buttons, chips,
  nav labels, captions.
- **Label XS (11px / 600, uppercase, +4% tracking):** Eyebrow-style section
  headers (e.g. the "OCTOBER 2026" month divider in the transactions list).
- **Weight variants**, same size and tracking as their base role. A suffix
  always means the same weight: Strong 600, Bold 700, ExtraBold 800,
  Black 900.
  - `body-md-strong` (600) - a touch heavier without reading as bold (links,
    row descriptions).
  - `body-md-bold` / `body-sm-bold` / `label-sm-bold` (700) - the common
    "this value matters" weight: form labels, button text, list-row
    emphasis. The most frequently used step in the app.
  - `label-lg-bold` (700) - transaction amounts, nav labels, confirm
    buttons.
  - `headline-md-strong` (600) - section headers within a screen ("Recent
    transactions", "Cash Flow").
  - `headline-lg-extra-bold` (800) - the most common title weight: dialog
    titles, form sheet headers, card titles.
  - `headline-sm-extra-bold` (800, 24px) - an alternate large title used by a
    handful of dialogs/cards instead of `headline-lg-extra-bold`.
  - `amount-display-extra-bold` (800, 28px) - the large numeric figure shown
    while editing an amount (transaction/wallet balance input).
  - `headline-display-black` (900, 28px) - reserved for the single
    biggest number on a screen: the dashboard's total balance figure,
    specifically. Not general emphasis.
  - `body-sm-strong` (600) - an unselected choice beside a bold one, and
    the name on the front wallet card peeking behind the balance.
  - `body-md-extra-bold` (800) - a figure that must win its line, e.g. the
    balance after a wallet's type.
- **Styles named for one job**, so a screen never sets a size or weight:
  - `wallet-card-balance` (16px / 800) - the balance on a wallet card.
  - `month-pill` (13px / 500) - an unselected month; the selected one is
    `body-md-bold`, a step larger.
  - `receipt-line` / `receipt-line-bold` (12.5px / 400, 700) and
    `receipt-total` (15px / 700) - the month receipt's own print scale.
  - `receipt-header` (11px, +0.8px tracking) - the receipt's header.
  - `chart-axis` (11px) - the cash flow chart's month labels.

### Principles

- Weight only ever escalates along this named ladder (`body-md` →
  `body-md-strong`, `label-lg` → `label-lg-bold`, etc.) - never a bespoke
  `fontWeight: 650` picked by eye for one screen.
- Negative tracking scales with size: the biggest display text gets the most
  negative tracking (-2%), small labels get the least.
- Color carries emphasis for amounts (`primary-strong` positive,
  `error` negative) so weight and color are never both doing the same job on
  the same piece of text.

### Large text

Waldo follows the phone's text size up to iOS's largest (AX5), the way
Apple's own apps do: everything gets big, but stays in order.

- **Titles grow less than body text.** iOS gives Flutter one factor for
  every size; `DynamicTypeScaler` applies Apple's per-style table instead,
  so at AX5 a title is only a little bigger than the text under it. Android
  14+ already scales this way itself.
- **Bars and segmented controls stop at xxxLarge** (`barMaxScale`), as
  iOS's tab bar and segmented control do: the navbar, the Expense/Income
  toggles, the undo countdown.
- **At the accessibility sizes (AX1 and up) layouts stack.** A badge moves
  above its text (`BadgeRow`), a value or button moves under its label,
  the wallet type tiles become one column, dialog buttons stack, and the
  wallet cards grow while the part under the next card stays the same.
- **No word is ever broken or cut.** One-line text wraps at accessibility
  sizes (`WholeWordsText.singleLine`), and a word wider than its line
  shrinks just enough to fit, like UIKit's `adjustsFontSizeToFitWidth`.
- **Amounts are never cut.** A balance or amount shrinks to fit its line
  instead.

## Layout

Two spacing systems, intentionally: a **page scale**
(`xs` 8 / `sm` 16 / `md` 24 / `lg` 32 / `xl` 48) for gaps between
sections, cards, and list rows, and a **component scale**
(`xxs` 4 / `component` 12) for the internals of small, dense widgets - pill
toggles, filter chips, dialog action rows - where the page scale would read
as too loose. `page-margin` (24px) is the fixed side margin on every screen.

Every screen uses the same 24px outer gutter; nothing goes full-bleed except
photographic wallet-card textures and chart canvases.

Round icon discs (badges) beside a label come in three sizes, `BadgeSize`
in `core/theme/badge_size.dart`: `sm` 28 (category chips in the
transaction form), `md` 36 (form fields, option tiles, category and list
rows) and `lg` 40 (settings rows, option-sheet rows, transaction rows).
A divider that starts under a row's title indents by row padding +
badge + gap, so it moves with the badge.

A badge's disc shows what is behind its card, as if cut out of it: the page
(`surface-container`) for a card on the page, the sheet (`surface`) for a
field or option row inside a sheet. The icon carries the meaning and stays
at 3:1 or more against the disc; the disc itself is decoration. An ink
(`primary`) disc is kept for a picked option or a focused field.

## Elevation & Depth

Waldo is almost entirely flat - most surfaces sit directly on the page with
no shadow at all, separated only by the `surface` / `surface-container` color
step. Two levels of soft, large-spread shadow exist for content that truly
needs to lift off the page:

| Level | Treatment | Use |
|-------|-----------|-----|
| None | No shadow | Default - list rows, chips, inputs, the vast majority of surfaces |
| Low | `rgba(0,0,0,0.04)`, offset `(0, 4)`, blur `24` | The nav bar, floating over the pages as they scroll behind it (cards stay flat) |
| High | `rgba(0,0,0,0.12)`, offset varies | Sheet/dialog shadowColor, drag handles |

There is no hard, tight shadow anywhere in the system - every shadow is a
large, low-opacity spread, closer to "the card is resting slightly above the
page" than a directional drop shadow.

## Shapes

Rounding is deliberately sparse - four steps, with a large gap between the
small and large tiers so the UI reads as either "a small control" or
"a soft card," never in between:

| Token | Value | Use |
|-------|-------|-----|
| `sm` | 12px | Chips, small badges |
| `md` | 20px | Text fields, segmented controls |
| `lg` | 28px | Cards, dialogs; a sheet when the screen is square |
| `pill` | 9999px | Buttons, nav pill, FAB, toggle tracks, tags |

The middle ground most systems have (8-16px) is intentionally absent.

## Borders

**Stitch, never solid.** Waldo draws borders as a dashed stitch, the leather
wallet motif, with `DashedBorder` (`core/widgets/dashed_border.dart`). Every
stitched shape - border, badge ring, pouch, button - cuts its dashes with
`dashPath` (`core/utils/dash_path.dart`), so none carries its own loop.

The thread and its patterns are tokens in `Stitch` (`core/theme/stitch.dart`):
one width (1.5px) and four dash/gap patterns that grow coarser with the
shape - `fine` 3/2.5 inside pills, `regular` 4/3 on borders and badge rings,
`receipt` 5/4 on the receipt's rules, `coarse` 6/5 on the pouch.

- **Selected and focused** states use an ink stitch (`primary`).
- **Outlines and placeholders** use a faint stitch (`on-surface-variant` at low
  alpha).
- Nothing has a border at rest unless it is a placeholder.
- **Sheets and dialogs have no frame stitch.** The stitch marks what's selected
  inside them, so a stitched frame would compete with it.
- **Errors are the one solid border** (`error`), so a broken field differs from a
  selected one by line as well as color.

## Motion

Durations are Material 3's own steps, named for what they move, in `Motion`
(`core/theme/motion.dart`). A new animation picks one of these instead of a
number of its own.

| Token | Duration | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `toggle` | 150ms (`short3`) | A segment or toggle filling in |
| `scroll` | 300ms (`medium2`), ease-out | A row scrolling to a picked item |
| `enter` | 350ms (`medium3`), fast then settling | Something arriving, like the undo bar |
| `hint` | 1400ms, out and back | The nudge hinting that a row scrolls |
| `balanceTuck` | 300ms (`medium2`), ease-out | The balance tucking into the pouch; the dots land with `settleCurve` |
| `receiptPrint` | 600ms (`long4`), ease-out | The month's receipt feeding out from under the pouch |
| `cardFall` / `cardSettle` | 400ms (`medium4`) then 300ms (`medium2`) | A new wallet's card fading in (`cardDropFade` 100ms) as it falls `cardDropHeight`, tilted `cardTilt`, `cardSink` too deep into the pouch, then popping back |
| `pouchSquash` / `pouchRebound` | 100ms (`short2`) then 250ms (`medium1`) | The pouch giving to `pouchSquashScale` as the card lands, then springing back |
| `pillStretch` | 450ms (`long1`), `emphasizedCurve` | The month and navbar pills stretching to a new pick like ink: the front edge arrives in the first `pillStretchLead`, then the back catches up |
| `pillLabel` | 250ms (`medium1`) | A month or tab label's colour following the pill |
| `chartDraw` | 800ms (`extralong2`), `emphasizedCurve` | The cash-flow line drawing in, left to right; then its end point pops on the `wobbly` spring and the month's total rises `chartLabelRise` in (`chartLabel` 250ms, `chartLabelDelay` 100ms after the point) |
| `chartMorph` | 600ms (`long4`), `emphasizedCurve` | The line reshaping between expense and income, colour and all |
| `menuFadeIn` / `menuClose` | 100ms (`short2`) / 200ms (`short4`) | A ⋮ menu growing from `menuScaleFrom` out of its dots on `bouncyCurve`, fading in; closing is a plain fade. The dots dip to `menuDipScale` in `menuDip` (200ms) |
| `cardLeave` | 400ms (`medium4`), `cardLeaveCurve` | A deleted wallet's card sliding `cardLeaveDistance` of its width out; the cards below close the gap on `snappy` from `cardGapDelay` (250ms, `medium1`) |
| `cardReturn` | 300ms (`medium2`), `emphasizedCurve` | Undo: the card fading back from `cardReturnScale` while the cards below make room |
| `cardLandFade` | 100ms (`short2`) | A new wallet's card landing in the stack from `cardLandHeight` above at `cardLandScale`, on `snappy`, while the cards below make room |
| `sheetExit` | 200ms (`short4`) | The new-wallet sheet sliding away; a card made in it waits this out before it lands |

Curves come from `Motion` too, and so do springs, named by feel with the
design playground's values (`snappy`: firm, a hint of overshoot; `bouncy`: one
clear bounce; `wobbly`: two small bounces). `SpringCurve` turns one into a
curve and its settle time, for APIs that take a duration and a curve. Simple
chains (fade, slide, shake) use `flutter_animate` and physical motion a
`SpringSimulation` on a `Motion` spring, with every duration from `Motion`,
never a `300.ms` of its own; the design guardrail catches a raw `.ms`,
`Duration(milliseconds:` or `Curves.`.

**Reduce motion:** every animation takes its duration through
`context.motion(Motion.x)`, which is zero when the phone asks for reduced
motion (iOS "Reduce Motion", Android "Remove animations"), so it jumps to its
end. A `flutter_animate` effect would still draw its first frame, so with
reduced motion it isn't built at all (`context.reduceMotion`). Anything that
moves on its own, like the scroll hint, is skipped.

Animate position, opacity, scale and paint only - never width, height or
padding, which lay the screen out again on every frame. A moving selection
is painted (`StretchPill`), not a resized box. The one exception is the
navbar: a tab's name opens and closes with its padding.

**Haptics** aren't motion, so they stay on with reduce motion:
`HapticFeedback.selectionClick()` when a choice moves (a month, a tab, the
cash-flow series), and only when it actually changes; `lightImpact()` when
something opens or lands (a ⋮ menu, a new card); `mediumImpact()` when
something goes away (a deleted card).

## Other tokens

Every design value a widget uses comes from `lib/core/theme`; there is no
`// one-off` escape hatch.

- **`Alpha`** (`alpha.dart`): opacity steps - `faint` 0.06, `tint` 0.08,
  `soft` 0.12, `medium` 0.18, `stitch` 0.3, `line` 0.4, `muted` 0.6,
  `strong` 0.8.
- **`ComponentSize`** (`component_size.dart`): dimensions that belong to one
  component - the wallet card, the receipt, the chart, the undo ring, the
  navbar's selected tab, the grabber.
- **`FinishColors`** (`finish_colors.dart`): the metal and leather colours,
  which imitate real objects and so don't follow the themes.
- **`Fades`** (`fades.dart`): alpha masks that fade a row's edges.

Two kinds of number stay in code: the shape of a drawing inside its painter
(the pouch's curve, the eye's path, the barcode's bars), and behaviour
constants such as counts and time windows (five category rows, the 5-second
undo window).

## Components

- **Buttons (primary):** `DashedStitchButton` - a `primary`-filled pill
  (`rounded.pill`) with an `on-primary` dashed stitch just inside its edge,
  `on-primary` text in `label-lg-bold`, 24px x 16px padding (16px x 8px
  and `body-sm-bold` when `compact`). Plain `FilledButton`s get the same
  pill and type from the theme.
- **Buttons (secondary):** `SecondaryButton` - a `rounded.pill` filled with
  `on-surface` at `Alpha.faint`, the month selector arrows' grey, on the page
  and on sheets alike; `on-surface` text in `body-md-strong`, 12px x 4px
  padding. For low-emphasis actions beside a section or sheet title ("See
  all", the filter's "Reset").
- **Cards:** one widget, `SurfaceCard`: `surface`-filled, flat; a card of
  one row is a `pill` like the buttons and fields, a card of several keeps
  `rounded.lg` (a tall pill rounds into a stadium). Every card of rows uses it (settings, categories, transactions), and
  its rows sit 16px (`sm`) from the sides with 12px above and below a
  40px (`BadgeSize.lg`) disc; hairlines start under the text.
- **Sheets:** every bottom sheet opens with `showAppSheet` and floats: a
  `surface` card 12px (`component`) in from the screen's sides and bottom (just
  above the keyboard when it's up). Its corners are concentric with the
  screen's: the phone's own corner radius, read at start by
  `corner_radius_plugin`, less those 12px; `rounded.lg` when the phone doesn't
  say. Inside, content keeps a 24px (`md`) side margin, form or list alike.
- **Input fields:** `field-fill` background (never tinted toward the cream
  palette), `field-border` outline, `rounded.md`, `body-md` typography,
  2px `primary` border when focused, `error` border (1.5-2px) in an error
  state.
- **Required fields:** the label ends in " *" (`FieldLabel(required: true)`),
  in `on-surface` rather than `error` - nothing is wrong yet. Screen readers
  hear "required". Optional fields carry no mark, and neither do the hero amount
  and single-field forms.
- **Sheet errors:** a failed save shows its message inside the sheet, centered
  just above the button, in `body-md-strong` `error` - never a SnackBar, which
  would land behind the sheet. It names the cause (storage full, try again)
  when it is known.
- **List rows (transactions/wallets):** leading icon in a circular tint of
  the row's semantic color (income/expense/wallet type), `label-lg-bold`
  title, `body-sm` muted caption, trailing amount in `label-lg-bold` colored
  by `primary-strong` (income) or `error` (expense).
- **Wallet cards:** a photographic metal texture (light mode) or a true
  dark-metal gradient (dark mode) - never a flat themed color - with
  `leather` ink on the photo and `on-leather` ink on the dark metal.
- **Balance pouch and undo bar:** `leather` fill, `on-leather` text, the same
  in both themes.
- **List rows (secondary caption):** `surface` background, `on-surface-variant`
  caption/subtitle text, `body-sm` typography.
- **Amounts:** `primary-strong` text for income, `error` text for expense,
  both at `label-lg-bold`.
- **App bar:** transparent background - it never visually separates from the
  page.
- **One pattern per input:** the same field looks and works the same on
  every screen. The amount is always `TransactionAmountCard`: the
  Expense/Income toggle (the picked side a `surface` pill with the ink
  stitch) over a `FormPillField`, symbol in the disc, sign before the number,
  code at the end, typed with the system keyboard. A choice from a list
  (wallet, category, date) is always an `EditFieldRow` - name at the start,
  value at the end - grouped in one `surfaceContainer` card by
  `EditFieldRow.group`, opening `showOptionSheet` - the same tiles as the
  Settings pickers, picked with one tap. A picker that can grow puts its
  "New ..." tile first, so making one never means leaving the sheet. A
  sheet of things to do (the dashboard's "Add") is `showActionSheet`: the
  same title, close button and tiles, without the rings.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Use `surface-container` as the page background and `surface` for anything
  that should lift off it by one step - never introduce a third surface
  level.
- Reserve `error` strictly for destructive actions and negative/expense
  amounts.
- Use `primary-strong` only on a plain surface for a single emphasized
  element (an amount, a link, a marker) - not as a button fill.
- Escalate text weight only along the named ladder (Strong, Bold,
  ExtraBold, Black), never with an inline `fontWeight`.
- Keep the leather pouch and undo bar `leather`/`on-leather` in both themes.
- Reuse the input a screen already has before building another: a new form
  uses the same amount card and pickers as the existing ones.

### Don't
- Don't add a second accent hue - orange stays the only color in the system.
- Don't use `field-fill`/`field-border` anywhere except form inputs; they're
  deliberately the one cool-gray note against an otherwise warm palette.
- Don't round anything to a value between 12 and 20 - the gap between `sm`
  and `md` is intentional.
- Don't add a shadow to a surface that doesn't need to visually group or
  float - flat is the default, not the exception.

## Known Gaps

- **Color role scope:** Waldo's palette intentionally covers 14 roles and
  does not define Material 3's `secondary`, `tertiary`, `outline`, or the
  `surface-container-low/high/highest` tiers - the product has no use for a
  second or third accent hue, and only needs the one surface step documented
  above. If a future screen needs one of these roles, add it deliberately
  rather than letting it fall back to a Material default.
- **`on-error`:** resolved without a new token - every filled-`error`
  surface (the destructive dialog button, the selected Expense segment) uses
  `colors.onPrimary`. With the light `error` at `#C44100` that is 4.51:1
  (it was 4.11:1 at `#CF4500`, under AA); dark is 6.43:1.
  `ColorScheme.onError` is mapped the same way in `app_theme.dart`.
- **Strong-weight token reconciliation is done.** All ~90 hardcoded
  `fontWeight:` call sites now go through a
  named `context.*` getter (`lib/core/theme/app_theme.dart`). The original 4
  tokens only covered `bodyMedium`/`labelLarge`/`titleLarge`/`headlineLarge`
  bases; the real distribution skewed toward other Material roles
  (`titleMedium`, `headlineSmall`, `headlineMedium`, `bodySmall`,
  `labelSmall`) and toward `700` more than `600`, so the set grew to the
  13 tokens now in the front matter - `bodyMdBold`/`bodySmBold`/
  `labelSmBold` (the most common step, `700`), `headlineMdStrong`/
  `headlineSmExtraBold`/`amountDisplayExtraBold`, plus a correction:
  `headlineDisplayBlack` was originally defined against `headlineLarge`
  (32px, unused anywhere in the app) when the dashboard balance figure it's
  for actually uses `headlineMedium` (28px) - fixed to match the real call
  site rather than inflate the figure's size.
  No raw `fontWeight:` or `fontSize:` is left outside `lib/core/theme`.
- **Elevation has no numeric scale.** `Elevation.low`/`.high` (BoxShadow
  lists) and `.highShadowColor` are live, but the handful of raw Material
  `elevation: 1` / `elevation: 3` doubles (`circle_icon_button.dart`,
  `wallets_card_stack.dart`) aren't tied to a named constant yet.
- **Guardrail doesn't check `EdgeInsets`/`SizedBox` numbers.**
  `make design-guardrail` fails on a raw `Color(0x...)`, `Colors.*`,
  `fontSize:`, `fontWeight:`, `Duration(milliseconds: ...)` or fractional
  `alpha:` outside `lib/core/theme`, with no exception marker. Spacing and
  sizes come from `Spacing`, `BadgeSize`, `IconSize` and `ComponentSize`
  today, but a numeric check would also flag painter geometry, so it isn't
  on.
- **A lighter orange for negative balances on dark metal.** In dark mode a
  wallet card's negative balance uses `error` lifted 18% in lightness
  (about `#F8B08F`, `walletCardNegativeColor` in `wallet_card_finish.dart`),
  not `error` itself: on the metal's lighter shine, plain `error` drops to
  about 1.7:1 and the lifted one reaches about 2.6:1. It is still the one
  orange hue, only lighter, and it appears nowhere else; the lift is
  `FinishColors.negativeOnMetalLift`. Light mode uses plain `error`.
- **Figma:** no Figma file yet (Phase 4 of the design-system spec).
